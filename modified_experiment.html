2. Google Sheets Setup & Data Table Layout

Prepare your Google Sheet data logging file prior to beginning laboratory testing. Follow the step-by-step instructions below to quickly build your table headers and time sequence.

Step-by-Step Google Sheets Setup Guide:
  1. Create a new spreadsheet on Google Sheets named Rate of Reaction - Modified Experiment Data.
  2. Merging Condition Header Cells:
    Click and drag your mouse to select cells B1:D1 for your first condition header (e.g., 0.1 M). Then, click the Merge cells icon ⊞ on the top toolbar (or hover over top menu and choose Format > Merge cells > Merge horizontally). Repeat this horizontally for each condition header across 3 columns per condition.
  3. In Row 2, set cell A2 to Time (s), and set cells B2 to P2 to repeat Trial 1, Trial 2, and Trial 3 under each condition header.
  4. Auto-Filling Time Numbers Down Column A:
    Type 0 in cell A3 and 30 in cell A4. Highlight both cells (A3:A4). Hover your mouse cursor over the small blue square (the Fill Handle) at the bottom-right corner of the highlighted selection until your cursor transforms into a plus symbol (+). Click and drag straight down to line 300 seconds to instantly auto-fill the whole time series.
    💡 Hover Tip: Double-clicking the blue plus (+) handle will also automatically fill down to match adjacent columns!
